# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output, `/en` and `/ja` routes), Tailwind, plain three.js loaded only where used. Hosted on Vercel with Vercel Analytics. Contact form posts to Web3Forms (no server).

## Users

Founders and decision-makers at startups and small-to-mid businesses, in Japan and internationally, who need software built and are evaluating whether Funkel Labs can be trusted to build it. They arrive from a referral, a shared link, or search, and decide within a minute whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Funkel Labs is a software development studio. The landing page exists to turn interest into a first conversation: the visitor gets in touch through the contact form. A "book a call" action is added only once a real Calendly/Cal.com link exists.

## Positioning

Open: the claim a neighbouring studio could not truthfully copy is not yet stated. Known so far: a small studio that takes products from idea to shipped, working in both English and Japanese.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare several studios side by side, often on a phone first and a laptop later. Japanese clients expect a native-quality Japanese page, not a machine translation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services (lead with the first): product builds / MVPs, web apps, mobile apps (iOS/Android), AI integration (LLM features, automation).
- Single scrolling page: hero, services, app showcase, how we work (Discover, Design, Build, Launch & support), about/team, contact, footer.
- Bilingual EN/JA. Claude drafts the Japanese; a native speaker on the team reviews it.
- three.js accents: a hero centrepiece plus subtle scroll-reactive ambience. Must degrade to a static image under `prefers-reduced-motion`, without WebGL, and on low-power devices; 3D loads after content.
- Undecided / TODO: domain, contact inbox address, app showcase details, team bios, legal entity name and address.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Funkel Labs. "Funkel" is German for sparkle; the name may be played on, lightly.
- No logo exists yet. The page uses a typographic wordmark with a small replaceable spark glyph.
- Personality: playful with a technical edge; confident, not corporate.

## Evidence on Hand

- A few apps the studio has built, to be showcased (details and screenshots pending from the user).
- Absent: client logos, testimonials, metrics, case-study numbers. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Show the work before describing the process.
- Real people and real projects over claims.
- Equally at home in English and Japanese.
- Delight never costs readability or speed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-reachable everything, reduced-motion respected, page fully readable with 3D off.
