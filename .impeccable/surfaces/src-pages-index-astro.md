# Landing page (EN / JA)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: founders and SMB decision-makers, Japan + international. Action: get in touch (contact form). Proof: the studio's own apps (pending), real team. Constraints: bilingual, three.js with static fallback, no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every shipped product is a print run: two inks, registered by hand, made to be handed over. Refuses the dark-page-with-neon-accent-and-glowing-orb arrangement this category ships.

OWN-WORLD: Bright uncoated stock, no black. A medium blue drum sets every word, rule and icon; a fluorescent pink drum lays every fill; purple prints only where they overlap (multiply). Grain where ink skips, one hero word a hair out of register. Cabinet Grotesk 800 heads over Literata body; Japanese in Dela Gothic One heads and Zen Kaku Gothic New body. 2px radius, no shadows; depth is overprint. One strict 8px drum grid governs every measure (raise, from ANSI Nightboard).

STORY: The visitor sees a studio that makes things by hand with care, understands the four services, sees the real apps (screenshots in full colour on the stock, framed in blue hairline), sees the process as separations, meets the people, and sends a message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark with spark glyph left, nav + pink "Get in touch" right. Left 7 columns: "We build software that sparks." at up to 96px (6rem, the craft-floor ceiling), blue, "sparks." misregistered with a pink copy; one sentence; pink primary + blue-outline secondary. Right 5 columns: the three.js spark alone (raise, from Accretion Disk), rendered as two halftone separations, pink and blue, slowly drifting in and out of register with the cursor. Static two-ink SVG under reduced motion / no WebGL.

FORM: Two-Drum Riso, candidate 6 of 7 (assigned). seed:08f723d0fbce8d25. Signature interaction: the spark's plates register as you move toward it. "How we work" shows blue plate, pink plate, registered sheet (raise, from Sampler Proof). Form states print as stamped lines in the drum, not toasts (raise, from Phosphor Terminal).
